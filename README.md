# Ocean Tides

Tide predictions for macOS — high and low tides, weekly charts, moon phases,
sunrise and sunset times, for thousands of stations worldwide.

**[Download the latest release →](../../releases/latest)**

---

## Features

- **Daily and weekly tide charts** with high/low water predictions
- **Moon phases**, illumination, and upcoming phase calendar
- **Sunrise and sunset times** with daylight duration and solar noon
- **Highs & Lows** table showing the day's extremes
- **Station map** — browse stations by country, or use your current location

## Requirements

- macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later
- Apple Silicon or Intel

## Installation

1. Download `OceanTides-vX.Y.Z.zip` from the [latest release](../../releases/latest)
2. Unzip and drag **OceanTides.app** to your `/Applications` folder
3. Right-click the app and choose **Open** (required once, to clear Gatekeeper)
4. The app will automatically check for updates and notify you when a new version
   is available

## Desktop Widget

Ocean Tides includes a macOS desktop widget showing upcoming tides at a glance.
To add it:

1. Launch Ocean Tides at least once
2. Right-click the desktop and choose **Edit Widgets**
3. Find "Ocean Tides" and drag the widget to your desktop
